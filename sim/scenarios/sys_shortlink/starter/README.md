# Short-link service — design brief

You are the **Systems Designer**. Nia (EM) asked for "something like bit.ly but
ours" for a marketing campaign. Talk to her (and Cole in SRE) before you fill
the design.

This starter is **mostly docs**. A `src/` folder is here for later if you want
to sketch an API or a capacity sheet — the grade is the design and how you
defend it, not production code.

## What to do
1. Open **Team Chat**. Ask about traffic, reporting, and what the URL must look like.
2. Read `docs/constraints.md` and `docs/current-system.md`.
3. Write your proposal in `DESIGN.md` (the template is already there).
4. **Submit** the design (patch or repo). The team will come back with review questions.
5. Defend the trade-offs in chat / mail. Do not just add every ask.

## Files
- `DESIGN.md` — your design (this is the submission).
- `docs/constraints.md` — what we already know we cannot spend.
- `docs/current-system.md` — today's stack.
- `src/` — optional; sketches welcome, not required.
